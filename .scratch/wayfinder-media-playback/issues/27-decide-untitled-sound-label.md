# Decide how an untitled Shared Sound is labeled

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 22, 24

## Question

Because users do not provide titles or descriptions, what generated label should the Trigger Control Panel show: a short sequence/code, recording time, an automatically inferred sound label, or no textual identity at all?

## Answer

Generate a neutral label in the form `声音碎片 #XXXX`, where `XXXX` is a short uppercase identifier derived from the immutable content identifier. Show the recording or submission time as secondary information when available.

The label is generated by the Cloud Media Service and returned by the Compact Content URL response. The Trigger Board does not invent or localize identifiers independently.

The first implementation does not call an AI model to classify or describe the sound. This avoids turning content publication into a model-dependent workflow.
