## Question

How is the Bluetooth Speaker selected for the first demo?

## Answer

Use one fixed Bluetooth Speaker. The Playback Board should reconnect automatically using a configured Bluetooth name, address, or stored pairing record rather than presenting a general-purpose speaker picker.

The exact speaker identity can be supplied during implementation and does not change the product architecture.
